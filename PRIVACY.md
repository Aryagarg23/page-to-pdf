# Page to PDF — Privacy

Page to PDF does not collect, store or send any personal data.

- The PDF is made inside your browser by Chrome's own PDF engine and saved to your computer.
- Nothing is sent to any server. The extension makes no network requests.
- Your settings (paper size, layout and so on) are saved with Chrome's sync storage so they follow your Chrome profile. They are never sent anywhere else.
- No analytics, no tracking, no accounts.
